Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. AllerLens has no sign-up and no sign-in. Your allergen profile and scan history are stored on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile → Clear local data, or delete the app. Because we don't store your data in the cloud, either one removes everything.

A dish was flagged wrong

Open the dish and tap Report wrong. AllerLens uses AI to read menus and it can make mistakes — always confirm ingredients and preparation with restaurant staff before you order, especially if your allergy is severe. AllerLens provides informational guidance and is not medical advice.

The scan didn't detect my menu

Scanning works best on printed menus in good light, with the whole menu in frame and the text roughly upright. If the camera can't focus, you can import a photo from your library instead.

Which languages are supported?

Menus can be translated into English or Spanish. The source language is detected automatically.
